## 2. What is Ixtlan? What healthcare software does it make, for whom, and what would make a solution attractive to them?

### Takeaway
Ixtlan Team d.o.o. (Ljubljana, since 1999) is a custom-software house for Slovenian state administration — not a hospital-information-system vendor. Its health footprint is national eZdravje: it is NIJZ's long-standing partner for the **zVEM patient portal and mobile app** and one of four contractors on the Ministry of Health's **eKarton** national health-record project (2026), which is built on openEHR + FHIR and adds patient questionnaires (PROM/PREM) and a remote-monitoring pilot inside zVEM.

### Inferences
- The premise "Ixtlan makes hospital information systems / has hospital clients" is **not supported**. All evidence points to national-level public-sector systems; in health, the patient-facing national channel (zVEM) for NIJZ and the Ministry of Health. A pitch framed as "plugs into a hospital's HIS" misses their vantage point; "a module that could live inside zVEM / zVEM+ and write to CRPP" matches it.
- The challenge title ("monitoring patients at home after hospital discharge") is close to the zVEM roadmap item "Spremljanje pacientov na daljavo" (currently a diabetes pilot with manual and device data capture) and to eKarton's PROM/PREM questionnaire framework. Ixtlan is plausibly scouting ideas and talent for exactly this product line. This link is my inference; no source says Ixtlan authored the challenge for that reason.
- Likely resonance points for Ixtlan judges, derived from the above (inferred, not stated by them):
  - Patient side shaped like a zVEM micro-frontend/module (login via national identity is out of hackathon scope, but saying "this would sit behind zVEM login" is credible).
  - Staff side shaped like a zVEM+ dashboard for the care team (family-medicine team / patronažna sestra), since eKarton sends questionnaires only "where a team monitors them" — an explicit answer to "who watches the alerts?" matters.
  - Data modelled as FHIR resources (Questionnaire/QuestionnaireResponse, Observation) or openEHR archetypes, destined for CRPP — these are the standards named in the national project.
  - Usability for ages 18–75 (the stated zVEM design constraint) — relevant for stroke/older patients using browser games.
  - "Enter once, use many times": game-derived motor scores as structured, reusable observations rather than free text.
  - Manual entry + device capture are the two modes in the national pilot; browser-game motor measurement is a third, device-free objective mode — a differentiator.
- Ixtlan's own stack is Java/C# + TypeScript + Oracle/MS SQL with microservices. The hackathon stack does not need to match, but clean API boundaries and a relational data model will read as familiar.
- Ixtlan's exact eKarton lot(s) are not published in the sources read; its listing as the "+1" beside the three clinical-module vendors, plus its history as zVEM maintainer, suggests it handles the zVEM framework/integration side. Unconfirmed.
- Both job families Ixtlan recruits for (developers; analyst/designers) and its student open day suggest hiring is one motive for sponsoring; a team that shows requirements thinking (clinical workflow, data model) as well as code fits both profiles.

## 5. What do published guides/analyses say judges reward in digital-health hackathons?

### Takeaway
Published health-hackathon rubrics converge on the same handful of things: a real, evidenced clinical problem; plausible impact and feasibility; a prototype that actually functions with a usable interface; and a clear, well-structured pitch. Rubrics split roughly half concept, half execution.

### Cited Findings
- Mount Sinai Health Hackathon scoring sheet: 10 criteria scored 1–5 (max 50). Concept: (1) clinical relevance & validity — important problem supported by literature, patient testimony or expert opinion; (2) potential clinical impact — magnitude and scale; (3) commercial potential/sustainability; (4) novelty vs existing solutions; (5) design concept — feasibility, ingenuity, elegance. Execution: (6) demo progress; (7) functionality (5 = completely functional); (8) user experience — usability and interface aesthetics; (9) integration of more than one technology; (10) technical sophistication in at least one area. — [icahn.mssm.edu PDF](https://icahn.mssm.edu/files/ISMMS/Assets/About%20the%20School/Sinainnovations/Hackathon/health_hackathon_scoring.pdf)
- Harvard HSIL Hackathon 2027 rubric and pitch tips: 3-minute pitch + 5-minute Q&A; 10 criteria × 0–2 points: problem definition & framing (defined *and measured*), value proposition & innovation, opportunity (target users with evidence of need), competitive landscape, business model, go-to-market, ask, team, presentation, Q&A. Tips: hook within ~30 seconds; problem → solution → impact flow; avoid technical detail; be ready to say why it beats current practice, who uses it and who pays; "Aim for 80% accurate and 100% clear." — [hsph.harvard.edu PDF](https://hsph.harvard.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/HSIL-Hackathon-2027-Judging-Rubric-Pitch-Tips.pdf)
- Poncette et al., JMIR 2020 (Berlin health hackathons case study; dated 2020): jury of five (entrepreneur-physician, designer, two developers, a patient) scored on innovation potential and feasibility; execution at the event; multidisciplinary team; design and UX of the prototype; presentation. Final format in the example schedule: 3-minute demo pitch + 2 minutes Q&A. Authors recommend a systems approach: identify the problem, view it from each actor's perspective, propose a solution, get early user feedback. — [jmir.org/2020/3/e17004](https://jmir.org/2020/3/e17004)
- AdaHack-specific signals: 2025 judges praised "usability and technical execution"; 2026 pitch workshop stresses "structuring the story" and "key points that grab the jury's attention". — [abelium.com](https://abelium.com/en/initiatives/adahack/); [hack.klub-ada.si/en/timeline](https://hack.klub-ada.si/en/timeline)

### Inferences
- Mapping the common criteria onto this team's project (my synthesis, for the 3-minute format):
  - *Clinical relevance, evidenced*: one sentence with a cited number on post-discharge deterioration going unnoticed between visits; name the three conditions.
  - *Impact*: earlier detection → earlier intervention; say who acts on an alert and what they do.
  - *Feasibility*: runs in a browser, no hardware; name the national rails it would ride on (zVEM/zVEM+, CRPP, FHIR/openEHR).
  - *Functionality + UX*: live end-to-end loop — patient plays a game → score → trend → clinician alert. A working loop scores on four of Mount Sinai's five execution criteria at once.
  - *Novelty*: objective motor measurement via games vs. questionnaire-only PROMs and vs. the national pilot's manual/device entry.
  - *Presentation*: hook in 30 s, problem → solution → impact, one patient story.
- Business-model/go-to-market criteria (Harvard-style) probably weigh less at a community hackathon with public-sector sponsors than at an investor-style event; "who pays" here is the public payer/national platform, which is Ixtlan's actual customer base.
- With a 2-person team, "multidisciplinary team" can be addressed by showing clinical grounding (literature, validated scales, any clinician input) rather than headcount.
- Honest handling of ML limits (no clinical validation, synthetic/own data, per-patient baseline rather than diagnosis) is likely to read as credibility to a vendor that builds regulated public systems; over-claiming diagnostic accuracy is the main credibility risk.

### Gaps
- AdaHack 2026's own rubric is not published (see section 1), so the above are general patterns from other health hackathons (two US academic events, one German case study), not AdaHack's criteria.
- A broader systematic review table of health-hackathon judging criteria (PMC7872325) could not be read (bot check), so no frequency data on which criteria are most common.
- No Slovenia-specific analysis of what health-hackathon juries reward was found.

## 6. What do zVEM and zVEM+ concretely offer today, on what architecture/standards, what is planned for remote monitoring / PROMs / telemedicine, and how could a third-party module realistically plug in? (added on coordinator's instruction)

### Takeaway
zVEM (patient portal + mobile app, 700,000+ users) already has every "rail" a post-discharge module needs: national login, proxy access for relatives (zVEM pooblastila, since June 2025), secure messaging to the care team (communication hub, 2025), push notifications, and — under the "Zdravje na daljavo" and eKarton projects — digital questionnaires/PROMs stored in CRPP plus a remote-monitoring pilot (diabetes; manual and device data capture). zVEM+ is the staff-side portal inside the secure zNET network where team-level communication is configured and where eKarton modules will appear. No public third-party plug-in API or SDK for zVEM was found; the documented integration pattern is components built by contracted vendors as micro-frontends inside the zVEM framework, with data in CRPP modelled on openEHR and FHIR.

### Cited Findings

**zVEM — what patients get today (PRIMARY unless marked)**
- Views: test results/reports, e-prescriptions and dispensed medicines, e-referrals and appointments (book/cancel via eNaročanje, waiting times), sick-leave certificates (eBOL), other CRPP documents; 2023 redesign added a timeline view and the personal medicine card (OKZ); since 2025 ZZZS data (insurance, treatment costs, medical aids, EHIC). — [ezdrav.si/resitve/zvem](https://ezdrav.si/resitve/zvem/)
- Patients can see the audit trail of who accessed their data, prohibit access to their patient summary (PPoP) and record advance-directive statements. — [NIJZ eZdravje overview deck (2025 figures)](https://nijz.si/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Digitalizacija-zdravstva-in-storitve-eZdravja-v-Sloveniji-Hajdi-Kosednar.pdf)
- Login: SI-PASS (qualified certificate, biometric ID card, smsPASS; eIDAS for EU citizens). — [ezdrav.si/resitve/zvem](https://ezdrav.si/resitve/zvem/); [NIJZ deck](https://nijz.si/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Digitalizacija-zdravstva-in-storitve-eZdravja-v-Sloveniji-Hajdi-Kosednar.pdf)
- Mobile app: Android and iOS since 2021 (also Huawei per NIJZ); push notifications since Dec 2023 for new results, referrals, appointments, sick leave. — [ezdrav.si/resitve/zvem](https://ezdrav.si/resitve/zvem/)
- Contact data: since end of 2024 patients enter e-mail/phone for notifications and some documents. — [ezdrav.si/resitve/zvem](https://ezdrav.si/resitve/zvem/)
- Communication with the care team ("Komunikacijsko vozlišče", built under "Zdravje na daljavo", from 2025): secure messages to the personal doctor's practice — request an appointment, prescription, referral or sick leave, or ask about a result/referral; extension to specialists and other teams is planned. — [ezdrav.si/resitve/zvem](https://ezdrav.si/resitve/zvem/)
- Rollout of that channel: about 25 health centres by 16 Jan 2026 (RTV SLO/STA, SECONDARY); 40+ providers and 1,250+ active health workers by March 2026 (NIJZ). Phone, in-person and postal contact must remain available. — [rtvslo.si](https://www.rtvslo.si/enostavno/uporabno/z-zdravnikom-se-bomo-pogovarjali-preko-portala-zvem/770299); [gov.si PDF](https://www.gov.si/assets/ministrstva/MZ/DOKUMENTI/2-NOVICE/03032026_Konferenca-eZdravje-NOO-2026/Zdravje-na-daljavo-in-zVem.pdf)
- User numbers (sources differ by date and definition): 560,000 active users / 360,000 on the app (NIJZ deck, 2025 figures); ~605,000 registered and 380,000 activated app users on 23 Feb 2026 (NIJZ, March 2026); "over 700,000 portal users in 2026", 900,000+ including linked persons, and "more than 500,000" app activations (ezdrav.si product page); 700,000+ in the sponsor brief. — [NIJZ deck](https://nijz.si/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Digitalizacija-zdravstva-in-storitve-eZdravja-v-Sloveniji-Hajdi-Kosednar.pdf); [gov.si PDF](https://www.gov.si/assets/ministrstva/MZ/DOKUMENTI/2-NOVICE/03032026_Konferenca-eZdravje-NOO-2026/Zdravje-na-daljavo-in-zVem.pdf); [ezdrav.si/resitve/zvem](https://ezdrav.si/resitve/zvem/)
- "zVEM digi točke" (assisted-access help points) are being opened in health centres through 2026. — [gov.si](https://www.gov.si/novice/2026-01-16-po-zdravstvenih-domovih-zvem-digi-tocke-za-pomoc-pri-urejanju-dostopa-do-zvem/)

**Relatives / caregivers: proxy access ("zVEM pooblastila") (PRIMARY)** — [NIJZ/eZdravje leaflet, Dec 2025](https://ezdrav.si/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/zVEM_pooblastila_A5_Dec2025_TRE_V6-1.pdf)
- Legal basis: Pravilnik o zVEM pooblastilih (Uradni list RS 34/25); live since June 2025 per the zVEM product page. The authorisation is stored in CRPP.
- Principal: a patient aged 15+. Proxy: an adult. Parents see children under 15 and guardians their wards without this mechanism (NIJZ deck).
- What the proxy can do: log in to portal and app; **communicate with the chosen doctor and other health workers**; view e-prescriptions/dispensed medicines; view, book and cancel appointments; see ZZZS data; view health records (PPoP, outpatient reports, other documents); see the audit trail.
- What the proxy cannot do: object to PPoP access; make special declarations of will under the Patients' Rights Act; grant further authorisations (NIJZ March 2026 slides). All proxy actions are logged.
- How it is set up: by the patient in the zVEM **portal** ("Upravljanje pooblastil"; not in the mobile app); in person at ZZZS or NIJZ units; at healthcare providers — explicitly including **during home visits and community-nursing (patronaža) visits** for patients with limited mobility; or by post with a notarised signature. Valid until revoked or for a fixed term.
- Stated purpose: helping less digitally literate patients. — [ezdrav.si/resitve/zvem](https://ezdrav.si/resitve/zvem/)

**zVEM+ — what health workers get today (PRIMARY)** — [ezdrav.si/resitve/zvemplus](https://ezdrav.si/resitve/zvemplus/)
- National portal for professional users only, introduced January 2021, running inside the secure zNET network; login with a qualified certificate or the ZZZS professional card certificate plus roles in the EueZ security scheme.
- Functions listed: configuring communication at organisation / team / hierarchy level **including working hours**; secure communication with patients and with other healthcare teams; managing patient and staff contact data; **multichannel notifications via an API**; advanced patient search; demographic queries (RDSP); lists for school preventive exams; CRPP reviewer queries; rare-disease register (RRNB) entry; creating/managing personal medication cards (OKZ); entering zVEM authorisations.
- Planned: eKarton modules "bodo na voljo" through zVEM+.
- Developer material exists for prerequisites/start-up and for the contact-data interface; nothing broader is described.
- Sponsor brief: 3,600+ healthcare workers use zVEM+. — `ixtlan_challenge_brief.md`

**Architecture and standards (PRIMARY)**
- National centralised platform run by NIJZ since 1 Dec 2015; participation mandatory for public and private providers; providers connect over zNET; 20+ vendors of local systems, 10+ connected; local systems use standardised APIs to core services; a central XDS document repository holds documents; patient identifier = health-insurance card number; staff identified via RIZDDZ, access governed by EueZ; SNOMED CT and ICD-10 in the patient summary. — [NIJZ deck](https://nijz.si/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Digitalizacija-zdravstva-in-storitve-eZdravja-v-Sloveniji-Hajdi-Kosednar.pdf)
- CRPP holds the patient summary (allergies, vaccinations, medicines, conditions, procedures, implants, recommendations…), PDF documents (specialist reports, **discharge letters**, lab, radiology, physiotherapy reports), ~200,000 new records/day, 330M+ documents; access by GP choice, referral, activity type or 45-day patient consent; every access audited. — [NIJZ deck](https://nijz.si/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Digitalizacija-zdravstva-in-storitve-eZdravja-v-Sloveniji-Hajdi-Kosednar.pdf)
- eKarton (2026) moves CRPP from documents to structured data: data model and codebooks based on **openEHR and FHIR**; HL7v3 for lab exchange; LOINC; zVEM (portal / mobile / plus) becomes a framework into which separately developed components are integrated with a **micro-frontends** approach — eKarton is the first project built this way. — [gov.si eKarton PDF, 3 Mar 2026](https://www.gov.si/assets/ministrstva/MZ/DOKUMENTI/2-NOVICE/03032026_Konferenca-eZdravje-NOO-2026/eKarton.pdf)
- IHE is not named in any deck read; the only IHE-family element visible is the "XDS" document repository.

**Published plans: remote monitoring, PROMs, telemedicine (PRIMARY)**
- "Zdravje na daljavo in telemedicina" project (Ministry of Health, RRF-funded; page last updated 26 Sep 2024): remote services via the zVEM app/portal; digital questionnaires replacing paper, with responses **including PROMs stored directly in CRPP so all treating staff can see them**; an interface connecting devices for **remote vital-signs monitoring** of enrolled patients; improved ePosvet starting with rheumatology; goals include reducing routine staff workload. — [gov.si](https://www.gov.si/zbirke/projekti-in-programi/zdravje-na-daljavo-in-telemedicina/)
- NOO deadline for Zdravje na daljavo: 30 Jun 2026; KPI of 1,500 health workers communicating with patients through it by 31 Dec 2025. — [NIJZ deck](https://nijz.si/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Digitalizacija-zdravstva-in-storitve-eZdravja-v-Sloveniji-Hajdi-Kosednar.pdf)
- Status March 2026 (NIJZ): "Spremljanje pacientov na daljavo" = pilot for diabetes patients with manual data entry replacing paper and capture from devices; "Zdravstveni nabiralnik" = surveys, declarations, satisfaction questionnaires, experience/outcome measurement, signed consents; audio-video consultations planned, with mandatory booking for that service in future. — [gov.si PDF](https://www.gov.si/assets/ministrstva/MZ/DOKUMENTI/2-NOVICE/03032026_Konferenca-eZdravje-NOO-2026/Zdravje-na-daljavo-in-zVem.pdf)
- eKarton questionnaire framework: PROM, PREM, standard questionnaires and signed declarations; sent automatically or manually, with reminders; sent **only where a team monitors them**; individual answers anonymous to clinicians, aggregate only (as described in the March 2026 slides). Community-nursing module: home visits, pressure-injury and fall-risk assessment, digital work orders; family-medicine module includes communication with patronage nurses. — [gov.si eKarton PDF](https://www.gov.si/assets/ministrstva/MZ/DOKUMENTI/2-NOVICE/03032026_Konferenca-eZdravje-NOO-2026/eKarton.pdf)
- eKarton is defined as the long-term store for data coming from "Zdravje na daljavo". — [gov.si](https://www.gov.si/zbirke/projekti-in-programi/ekarton-in-enotni-administrativni-podatkovni-model/)
- Existing national telemedicine services are clinician-to-clinician: Telekap (remote stroke diagnosis; 12 hospitals, ~100 consultations/month) and Teleradiologija. — [ezdrav.si/resitve](https://ezdrav.si/resitve); [NIJZ deck](https://nijz.si/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Digitalizacija-zdravstva-in-storitve-eZdravja-v-Sloveniji-Hajdi-Kosednar.pdf)

**Other Ixtlan health deliverables visible in the eZdravje catalogue (attribution partly SECONDARY)**
- COVID-era zVEM extensions — EU digital COVID certificate, vaccination booking — are listed as eZdravje solutions; Žurnal24 (Jun 2021) attributes both to Ixtlan Team. — [ezdrav.si/resitve](https://ezdrav.si/resitve); [zurnal24.si](https://www.zurnal24.si/slovenija/za-izdelavo-digitalnega-covidnega-potrdila-bodo-placali-kar-milijon-evrov-369049)

### Inferences
- What the national platform does **not** yet show publicly: post-discharge (hospital → home) monitoring, objective functional/motor measurement, per-patient trend alerts to a care team, or a role for the hospital team after discharge. The remote-monitoring pilot is diabetes/primary care with manual and device entry; questionnaire answers are shown to clinicians only in aggregate. The sponsor's challenge sits exactly in this gap. (Absence from the sources read, not a statement by Ixtlan.)
- A realistic "how it would plug in" story, using only mechanisms that exist or are officially planned:
  - *Patient*: a zVEM component (micro-frontend) behind SI-PASS login, reached from a push notification "your daily check-in is ready"; no separate account.
  - *Relatives*: no new permission model needed — an adult proxy with a zVEM pooblastilo can already log in, see records and message the doctor; the module would simply honour that authorisation (e.g. a daughter completes the check-in with or for a parent, sees the trend, gets the same warning). The authorisation can be arranged by the community nurse during a home visit.
  - *Data*: daily answers as questionnaire responses/PROMs and game results as structured observations written to CRPP (FHIR Questionnaire/QuestionnaireResponse/Observation or openEHR equivalents) — the storage path "Zdravje na daljavo" already defines for PROMs.
  - *Staff*: a zVEM+ module listing only patients whose trend deviates, routed by the team/hierarchy and working-hours configuration that zVEM+ already has; follow-up through the existing secure messaging channel rather than a new inbox. This directly answers the brief's "when and how does a human enter the process" and "staff time is limited".
  - *Hospital → primary care hand-off*: enrolment triggered by the discharge letter arriving in CRPP (discharge letters are already CRPP documents); visibility for GP, community nurse and hospital team follows existing CRPP access rules (GP choice, referral, activity).
- Individual-level alerting differs from the eKarton questionnaire rule that clinicians see answers only in aggregate. A monitoring module needs identified, per-patient data with explicit enrolment/consent — worth saying out loud, as the diabetes remote-monitoring pilot presumably already works that way.
- For the hackathon demo none of this needs to be built: a clearly labelled architecture slide ("demo: React + SQLite; target: zVEM component + zVEM+ module + CRPP via FHIR") is consistent with the brief's "integration not mandatory but a plus".
- The stroke scenario has a national hook: Telekap already covers acute remote stroke diagnosis in 12 hospitals, so "what happens after the stroke patient goes home" is a natural continuation — if the team narrows to one scenario, stroke has the strongest link to existing eZdravje services.
- Usability bar: zVEM's audience is 18–75 and the proxy mechanism exists because many patients are not digitally fluent; games and the daily check-in must work for an older post-stroke user or their proxy on a phone browser.
